FAQ

How do I log a set?

Open a workout, write the exercise name on a line, then type your sets underneath as reps × weight, for example 8x 80 for 8 reps at 80. The custom keypad keeps everything in thumb reach.

I made a typo. How do I fix it?

Tap the line and edit it, like in a notes app. Your history and charts update from the corrected workout. Automatic hints for likely typos are coming soon.

Kilograms or pounds?

Both. Pick your default unit in Settings. For a single set you can type the other unit, for example 8x 220 lb, without changing the default.

What are categories?

Labels like Push, Pull and Legs, each with a colour, so you can see what kind of day a workout was. You can rename them, add your own and change the colours.

Does the timer keep running if I switch apps?

Yes. The workout timer remembers when it started, so it shows the right time when you come back, without draining your battery in the background.

Can I bring my old logs?

ZnyprFit imports CSV files in its own format (from its own export or the earlier GymTrack app). Importing exports from other apps is not supported yet.

What happens when I change phones?

Two ways. Android Auto Backup can restore your workouts automatically to a new phone signed in to the same Google account. Or create a one-file backup in Settings, move the file, and restore it on the new phone. Backup files are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere safe.

Where is my data, and how do I delete it?

Only on your phone (and in your own Google account backup if you have that on). ZnyprFit has no internet access and no account, so I never see your data. Delete single workouts in the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything. Details in the privacy policy.
